Blue Hour — A Light Study

An interactive study of light on one building, Lantern House: a sculptural tower of twisting white slabs with a glazed lobby, ten residential floors and a penthouse pool. The scene opens at blue hour with the house dark, then the sky deepens and the house switches on, from the lobby up to the beacon. Scroll to fly a drone up the tower, drag to untwist the slabs, click a window to switch that floor's lights, and change the season or the weather.
